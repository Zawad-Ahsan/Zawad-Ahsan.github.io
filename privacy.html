Privacy

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and it sets no cookies of its own.

The contact form

When you send a message, the form collects your name, your email address, the subject and the message itself. A form delivery service called FormSubmit passes it to my inbox, so that service handles the message in order to deliver it.

I use what you send only to reply to you. I don't share it, sell it or add you to any mailing list.

Services this site relies on

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Fontshare. As with any website, both receive your IP address when your browser requests the files.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram and my projects take you to other sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your message

Email me at zawadoahsan@gmail.com and I will delete your message from my inbox.
